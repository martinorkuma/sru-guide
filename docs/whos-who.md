# Who’s Who at the SRU 

## Warrior Transition Support Program

The Army Reserve Warrior Transition Support Program (WTSP) helps Army Reserve Soldiers address administrative, financial, medical, and legal concerns during recovery and transition. 
Support Coordinators help clarify processes, troubleshoot obstacles, and coordinate issues requiring Army Reserve involvement.

Learn more about the [WTSP](https://www.usar.army.mil/WTSP/)

### When to contact your Reserve Support Coordinator

Contact your coordinator for help with:

- Reserve pay, orders, and personnel actions.
- Line of Duty (LOD) questions or processing concerns.
- Medical board questions, delays, or coordination issues.
- Unresolved concerns affecting your recovery or transition.
