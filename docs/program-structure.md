# Welcome to the Soldier Recovery Unit!

The SRU is broken down into four phases: 

1. SRU Evaluation: 
This is the Intake phase, where Soldiers are medically evaluated to assess if their injury/wound/illness/ meets the criteria for SRU entry or definitive care.  
90-day 12301(h) orders.  
Outcomes: Proceed to Complex Care, Remote Medical Management, or Return-to-Duty.  

2. Complex Care  
179-day 12301(h) orders.  

3. Remote Medical Management  
179-day 12301(h) orders.  

4. Veteran Track/Return-to-Duty
